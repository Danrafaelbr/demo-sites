Asked before booking

Do you take my insurance? +

We work with most major plans and bill them directly. Send us the name of your plan and we confirm before your visit.

I am scared of the dentist. Is that a problem? +

Not at all. About a third of our patients tell us the same. We go at your pace, stop whenever you raise a hand, and offer sedation for longer treatments.

How soon can I be seen? +

Usually within the week. Emergencies the same day.

Can I pay in instalments? +

Yes. Treatments above $500 can be split over up to twelve months with no interest.
